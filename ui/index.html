AirSend

Send your Windows audio to your HomePod

    Experimental: connecting another receiver during playback adds it to the current audio. Receivers may not be perfectly synchronized.

    Windows audio source

    Captures apps already playing on this output; it does not redirect them. Stop playback before changing it.

    Waits for audio and checks that capture keeps its signal. Restores the output if capture stops, and when playback stops or fails.

    Pairs and synchronization (experimental)

    Try simultaneous playback on two HomePods. Losing a member stops the whole group. Validation with real devices is pending.

    May reduce delay, but may also cause dropouts. Stop playback before changing it. The HomePod can add its own delay.

    Less delay More stable

    Confirm to apply a change. If audio is playing, the connection briefly restarts. 0 ms requests no extra buffer, but is experimental: it may fail or stutter. The receiver may add more delay.

    Audio diagnostics

    Download recent session metrics to compare delay and dropouts. Device names, IP addresses and credentials are excluded. Local measurements do not represent audible HomePod delay.

    Can't see your HomePod? Add its IP and port manually

    Enter an IP or IP:port, for example 192.168.1.50:7453. Use [address]:port for IPv6.
