AirPlay streaming

Delay

Less delay More stable

Applies to the current receiver. If audio is playing, the connection briefly restarts. Low values reduce the delay but may cause dropouts on a busy Wi-Fi.

Buffering

May reduce delay, but may also cause dropouts. Stop playback before changing it.

Connection

Audio capture

Windows audio source

Captures the apps already playing on this output; it does not redirect them. Stop playback before changing it.

Local output

Waits for audio and checks that capture keeps its signal. Restores the output if capture stops, and when playback stops or fails.

AirPlay receivers

Several receivers

Experimental: starting another receiver while one is playing adds it to the current audio instead of switching. Receivers may not be perfectly synchronized.

Stereo pairs and synchronization (experimental)

Try simultaneous playback on two HomePods. Losing a member stops the whole group.

Add a receiver by IP

If your HomePod does not appear (routers that do not propagate mDNS), enter its IP or IP:port, for example 192.168.1.50:7000. Use [address]:port for IPv6.
